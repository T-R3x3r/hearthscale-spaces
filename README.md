# Spaces

Pages you write, kept on this computer: headings, lists, checklists, quotes and code, typed as you would in Markdown. Share a page with someone on another computer and write it together.

## Get started

Open Spaces from the rail and choose **New page**. Give the page a title and write; Spaces keeps each change a moment after you stop typing. Your pages are listed in the sidebar, the last changed first.

Start a line with `#` and a space for a heading, `-` for a list, `1.` for a numbered list, `[ ]` for a checklist and `>` for a quote, and type three backticks for a block of code.

**Search pages** finds a page by its words. With an embedding model, it also finds a page by what it means.

To write a page together, open it and choose **Share page**, then **Copy link** and send the link to the other person. When they open it, Hearthscale asks them whether to join, and the page comes into their own Spaces. **Stop sharing** ends the sharing for everyone; each computer keeps its copy as an ordinary page.

## What Spaces asks for

- **Its own data.** Your pages stay in Spaces' own data on this computer.
- **Rooms with other computers.** A page you share goes only to the computers that join its room, and nothing else of yours goes with it.
- **Your clipboard.** **Copy link** writes the page's link to your clipboard.
- **An embedding model, if you have one.** Spaces uses it to search by meaning. Without one, the search uses the words alone.
